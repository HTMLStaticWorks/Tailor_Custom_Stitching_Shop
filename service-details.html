Men's Bespoke Suiting & Tuxedos

The Architecture of a Bespoke Suit

Unlike fused off-the-rack garments, a true bespoke suit is hand-cut from an individual paper pattern created exclusively for your body anatomy. Built with a full horsehair canvas core, the jacket naturally molds to your posture over time, providing exceptional breathability, longevity, and silhouette drape.

Key Features of Our Bespoke Construction

Floating Horsehair Canvas

Never glued or fused. Ensures unmatched flexibility and chest contouring.

Hand-Surged Buttonholes

Functioning sleeve buttonholes stitched with pure Italian silk thread.

Customized Interior Lining

Choose from solid Bemberg silk or vibrant custom printed silk interiors.

Hand-Stitched Lapel Roll

Pick-stitching along lapel edges provides signature Savile Row character.

Bespoke Process Timeline

We measure 35+ body points including posture incline, shoulder slope, and arm pitch. Select your fabric mill, lapel style, pocket configuration, and lining.

Try on the raw basted garment — assembled with temporary white thread without collar or lining — allowing the master cutter to refine waist compression and armhole height.

Final fitting verification. Garment is hand-pressed, steamed, and packaged in a complimentary breathable wooden hanger & garment travel case.
