Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers regarding measurement fittings, garment timelines, fabric selections, and alteration services.

Typically, a complete bespoke suit requires 3 to 5 weeks. This timeline includes your initial 35+ body anatomical measurement session, a basted fitting, hand-stitching canvas adjustments, and the final press fitting. Express expedited timelines (10 to 14 days) are available upon private consultation.

Bespoke garments are created from an entirely unique paper pattern drafted from scratch specifically for your body measurements, featuring full floating canvas construction and extensive hand-finishing. Made-to-measure adjusts a pre-existing standard pattern template.

Yes, we offer Cut, Make & Trim (CMT) services for patrons who supply their own luxury cloth. Our master tailors will inspect the fabric weave, weight, and drape before pattern layout to ensure optimal garment structure.

During an alteration session, our specialists pin the garment while you wear it to evaluate shoulder slope, sleeve length breaks, waist taper, and hem height. Turnaround time for standard alterations is 3 to 7 business days.

Yes, for corporate clients, executive groups, and bridal parties, our master cutters provide private home and office fitting sessions equipped with fabric swatch books and precision measurement kits.
